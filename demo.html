Interactive Solar System Animation

Welcome to our interactive solar system animation! This web-based simulation allows you to explore the planets of our solar system as they orbit around the sun.

Launch Solar System

Features

🪐 Realistic Orbital Motion

Each planet moves around the sun at a speed proportional to its actual orbital period.

🔍 Interactive Zoom & Pan

Use your mouse wheel to zoom in/out and click and drag to move around the solar system.

ℹ️ Planet Information

Click on any planet or the sun to view detailed information about it.

✨ Visual Effects

Beautiful background with stars, realistic planet colors, and Saturn's rings.

How to Use

Controls:

  • Zoom In/Out: Use your mouse wheel
  • Pan: Click and drag to move around
  • View Planet Info: Click on any planet or the sun
  • Toggle Orbits: Use the "Toggle Orbits" button
  • Toggle Labels: Use the "Toggle Labels" button
  • Reset View: Use the "Reset View" button

Planet Information:

Clicking on any planet or the sun will display a panel with the following information:

  • Name
  • Distance from Sun
  • Orbital Period
  • Diameter
  • Brief Description

Technology

This solar system animation is built using pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript - no external libraries or frameworks are needed. The animation is rendered directly in your browser using standard web technologies.
